# Visual quality review

Reviewed 24 October 2026.

## Corrections

The concept image connected the descender of **p** in Open to the upper-right serif of **M** in Markets. Separate raster traces then clipped the p and copied part of its serif into the M. The vector master now has a complete p descender, an independently drawn M serif, and increased vertical spacing in the stacked lockup. The touching t/s contours in Markets are also separated.

All six wordmark logo SVGs, their twelve PNGs and six vector PDFs use the corrected master. The seven application SVGs, seven editable templates, seven application PNGs, library preview, brand guide and intro video were regenerated. The horizontal logo canvas is now 1900 × 500 to preserve padding around the repaired descender and subtitle.

- Counted separate connected letters in rasterized master words: Open 4, Markets 7, Foundation 10. No unintended joins remain between those letters at the checked resolution.

- Rechecked file links, image dimensions, vector PDF structure and full video decoding. See [validation results](validation.json) and [wordmark results](wordmark-quality.json).
