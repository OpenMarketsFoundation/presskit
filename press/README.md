# Press information

## Name

Open Markets Foundation

## Positioning

Markets with integrity.

## Short description

Open Markets Foundation supports open infrastructure for markets that work for everyone, bringing builders and merchants together around open protocols for commerce.

## Longer description

Open Markets Foundation focuses on open infrastructure for commerce: tools and protocols that people can build on to buy, trade and sell. Its public resources include marketplace protocol specifications and open-source projects. The Open Markets Podcast, hosted by Eric FJ & Sync, explores commerce development around Nostr and Bitcoin through conversations with builders, merchants and users.
